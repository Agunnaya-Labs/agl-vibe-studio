CONFIGURE CUSTOM DOMAIN (aistudio.agunnayalabs.xyz):

Step 1: Go to Vercel Dashboard
  https://vercel.com/boom-annovation/agl-vibe-studio/settings/domains

Step 2: Add Domain
  - Click "Add Domain"
  - Enter: aistudio.agunnayalabs.xyz
  - Click "Add"

Step 3: Configure DNS
  Vercel will show two options:

  Option A: Nameserver (recommended)
    - Update your domain registrar's nameservers to:
      ns1.vercel.com
      ns2.vercel.com
  
  Option B: CNAME
    - Add CNAME record pointing to:
      cname.vercel.com

Step 4: Wait for DNS Propagation
  - Takes 5 minutes to 48 hours
  - Vercel auto-provisions SSL/TLS
  - Your domain will be live!
